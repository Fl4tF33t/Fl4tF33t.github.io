About Me

Hi, I'm Helmy Hussain, a game programmer with a focus on gameplay mechanics and artificial intelligence. I enjoy solving technical problems and turning ideas into robust gameplay systems.

My current work focuses on scalable AI and data-oriented systems using Unity's DOTS and ECS architecture, including behaviour trees and flow-field pathfinding.
